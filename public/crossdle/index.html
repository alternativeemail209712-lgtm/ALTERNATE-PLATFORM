❓ How to play CROSSDLE live

The goal

The host picks a word length (4-20 letters) and starts a round with a secret word of that length. Viewers guess by typing a matching-length word by itself in the LIVE chat (e.g. just APPLE for a 5-letter round, not "I think it's apple!"). Every valid word guessed gets tested and added to the board. First person to type the exact secret word wins the round.

The twist: a decoy word every guess

Here's what makes it tricky - after every guess, a fresh blue decoy word is also chosen. A tile turns green if the letter matches that position in the real answer OR the decoy. A tile turns yellow if the letter is somewhere in the answer or the decoy, just not that exact spot. So a green tile is a good sign, but it is not proof - it might only be green because of the decoy!

Example: guessing CLAIM while the decoy that round was CRIME
vs. decoy
  • C is green - matches the decoy's C in spot 1, so this alone doesn't confirm the real answer starts with C.
  • L is yellow - the decoy has no L at all, so this yellow can only be coming from the real answer (just not in spot 2).
  • A is grey - not in the answer or the decoy anywhere.
  • I is yellow - the decoy has an I (in a different spot), so this one's ambiguous.
  • M is green - the decoy's M is in a different spot, so this green must be from the real answer.

The on-screen keyboard

Above the board, every letter A-Z is colored with the best status seen for it so far this round - handy for spotting at a glance which letters are still worth trying.

Guessing & hints

  • Unlimited guesses, no clock - a round runs until someone solves it (or the host skips/reveals it). Take your time.
  • Only guesses that are real words at this round's length get tested and shown on the board - anything else shows a quick on-screen note explaining why, then gets out of the way.
  • With Strict fit on (the default), a guess must also still be a possible answer given the rows already on the board - because of the decoy, a row can be explained by the real answer plus some decoy word. A guess that no decoy could explain flashes a quick reason and is skipped. The host can switch Strict fit off in the host panel to accept any real word.
  • The host can drop a free hint letter any time, and one also appears automatically every few guesses.
  • If nobody solves it before time runs out, the answer is revealed and a new round begins.

Scoring & the leaderboard

  • +1 point just for making your first valid guess each round.
  • Solving it earns a bonus - bigger for fewer guesses, no hints used, and solving within the first few guesses overall.
  • Tap 🏆 Leaderboard up top any time to see the full top 10 as a list - the scrolling ticker up top always shows it too.
