Color shades

Choose how strong each tile color (red, orange, yellow, blue, purple and the green for a correct letter) looks, in the tiles, the legend and the keyboard. Ten shades per color, from lightest to darkest (the middle one is the standard look). It applies right away - no new round needed - and every screen connected to this game changes together.

Win celebration

After a win, the leaderboard pops up automatically, then closes on its own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

Starter word

On (default): one random word is placed on the board for free at the start of each round (worth no points) so chat has first alphabet-distance colors to read. Off: every round starts on a blank board. Takes effect from the next round; your choice is remembered.

Strict fit

On (default): a guess must still fit the colors of every guess on the board, otherwise it is skipped with the message below. The colors are very precise, so only a handful of words can fit after the first guess or two. Off: any real word of the right length is accepted and colored, so chat can probe freely. Your choice is remembered and applies right away.

Auto-color keyboard keys

On (default): every key on the keyboard takes the color its letter has earned on the board. Off: the keys stay plain (neutral) - viewers only read the colors on the tiles. Applies right away on every connected screen and is remembered.

Guess rejection message

Shown briefly whenever Strict fit skips a guess.

    How to play

    • Guess the hidden word — any length you like, chosen in Settings. Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime.
    • Each letter of a guess is colored by how far it is along the alphabet from the letter in the same spot of the hidden word — forward or backward. The numbers above the keyboard are the alphabet positions (A = 1 … Z = 26).
    • red 1–5 away   orange 6–10   yellow 11–15   blue 16–20   purple 21–25   green = correct letter in the correct spot.
    • Example: an orange M (13th letter) means the hidden letter is 6–10 steps away: forward S–W (19–23) or backward C–G (3–7).
    • A word already on the board is skipped. Strict fit is on by default, so only guesses that still fit every earlier color are accepted; the host can switch it off in Settings to accept any real word.
    • Keys turn green by themselves once that letter has been matched in the right spot in any guess. Everything resets each new round.
    • Scoring (Live): 1 point for every guess placed on the board, 5 points for solving the round.
    • 💡 unlimited hints suggest a word that best fits the colors so far (never the answer). Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how tricky the word itself is.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type guesses directly.
