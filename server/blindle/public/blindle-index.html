Scored on word familiarity, letter structure, and puzzle ambiguity — not just obscure vocabulary. "Random" skips scoring entirely and draws from every word of that length. Very short/long word lengths may not have every tier available.

Color shades

Choose how strong the green, yellow and red of the tiles and keyboard look. Ten shades per color, from lightest to darkest (the middle one is the standard look). It applies right away - no new round needed - and every screen connected to this game changes together.

Win celebration

After a win, the leaderboard pops up automatically, then closes on its own. Auto-continue (above) always waits for this celebration to finish before starting the next round.

Starter word

On (default): one random word is placed on the board for free at the start of each round (worth no points) so chat has green / yellow / red counts to read. Off: every round starts on a blank board. Takes effect from the next round; your choice is remembered.

Strict fit

On (default): a guess must still fit the green / yellow / red counts of every guess on the board - one that contradicts an earlier clue is skipped with the message below. Off: any real word of the right length is accepted and counted, so chat can probe freely. Your choice is remembered and applies right away.

Guess rejection message

Shown briefly whenever Strict fit skips a guess that conflicts with an earlier clue.

    How to play

    • Guess the hidden word — any length you like, chosen in Settings. Unlimited guesses, anyone can play, anytime.
    • Each guess only reveals counts: green = right letter & spot, yellow = right letter, wrong spot, red = not in the word. Never which letters — that's the puzzle.
    • With Strict fit on (the default), a guess is only accepted if it still fits every clue so far; one that contradicts an earlier clue flashes a quick reason and is skipped. The host can switch Strict fit off in Settings to accept any real word.
    • The keyboard (and each guessed word) is your own click-to-mark scratchpad — host only. The only thing the game colors for you: when a guess shows 0 green and 0 yellow, all its letters turn red automatically. Everything clears at the start of each new round.
    • 💡 unlimited hints suggest a word that fits the clues (never the answer). Difficulty (Normal/Medium/Hard/Random) sets how tricky the word itself is.
    • Live = real TikTok chat. Test = simulated chat, nothing saved. Offline = just you, type guesses directly.
